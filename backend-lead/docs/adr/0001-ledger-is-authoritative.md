# Ledger is the accounting authority for wallet funds

The wallet needs a fast balance check, while every change must remain reconstructible. The ledger is the accounting authority; the locked `wallet.balance` is the operational balance used to accept or reject a wager or withdrawal. Both update in the same database transaction. Each ledger movement has a unique business event key to guard against duplicate credits or debits. The ledger uses signed wallet movements rather than full double-entry accounting for this exercise. A rejected withdrawal returns funds through a new, uniquely tied compensating credit, never by altering its original debit. Reconciliation compares the operational balance with the ledger and blocks affected wallets on a detected mismatch. A discrepancy can affect decisions before detection, so reconciliation frequency and alerting are a production concern.
