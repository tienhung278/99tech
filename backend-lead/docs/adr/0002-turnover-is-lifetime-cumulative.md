# Turnover is lifetime cumulative

The exercise compares a member's total accrued turnover with total required turnover. We interpret both as lifetime cumulative values, so wagering before a later deposit can satisfy that deposit's requirement. Eligibility is calculated from durable completed deposits and accepted wagers rather than stored cumulative counters, keeping it auditable at the cost of larger queries as activity grows. The wager record, ledger debit, and operational balance update commit together. Deposit completion, wagering, and withdrawal eligibility all serialize through the wallet row; eligibility is queried after acquiring that lock. A per-deposit or non-carrying requirement could reduce abuse, but it changes the stated rule and needs a different accounting model; that risk should be called out in the submission's decision note.
